## What is FAFO?

Standard open-source licenses like MIT, Apache, or BSD are stuffed with polite corporate legalese that nobody reads.

**FAFO** cuts straight through the bullshit:
1. You are allowed to do **whatever the fuck you want** with the software.
2. If you deploy it to production without checking it and your infrastructure explodes... **you fucked around, and now you found out.**
3. You are strictly forbidden from coming crying to the maintainers.
